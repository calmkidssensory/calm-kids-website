About Our Mission

Calm Kids Sensory exists for the moments between the busy ones. Our books are built for children who feel things a little more intensely, and for the parents and educators looking for a quiet, screen-free way to help them settle.

Every page is kept simple on purpose. Wide lines, gentle subjects, and plenty of open space give overstimulated kids room to slow down, focus on one small task, and return to a calmer state at their own pace.

Through PikMe Publishing, we design each coloring book as a low-stimulus tool first, and a coloring book second, so it can sit alongside other sensory-friendly routines at home or in the classroom.

  • Low-stimulus, uncluttered page designs
  • Screen-free, hands-on focus activity
  • Sized and paced for sensory-sensitive kids
  • Made for home, therapy, and classroom use
